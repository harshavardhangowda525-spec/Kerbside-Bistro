About · Sadashiv Nagar, Bangalore

Made for good moments.

Kerbside Bistro is a neighbourhood cafe and restaurant on 3rd Main Road, Armane Nagar — open from breakfast through dinner, 9:00 AM to 9:15 PM.

The menu moves easily between cafe favourites and Continental, Italian, American and fast-food plates, with desserts and beverages to finish. Settle in indoors, bring the whole family — kids included — or take it home with takeaway and home delivery.
