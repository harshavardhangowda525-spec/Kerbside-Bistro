About Kerbside

Your neighbourhood bistro, made for good moments.

Kerbside Bistro is a relaxed café on 3rd Main Road, Armane Nagar, in the heart of Sadashiv Nagar.

Drop in for café favourites and coffee, or stay for Continental, Italian and American plates, fast food, desserts and beverages. Meet friends, bring the family, open your laptop or just slow down. We're open from 9:00 AM to 9:15 PM.
