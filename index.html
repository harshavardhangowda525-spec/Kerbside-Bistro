About · Non-veg biryani house

Biryani, the Gowdru way.

Donne Gowdru Biriyani Mane is a non-vegetarian biryani house in the Bengaluru donne tradition: seeraga samba rice cooked with whole spices, mint and green chilli, packed hot into a leaf cup.

Around the biryani sits the rest of a proper non-veg meal: chicken kabab fried to order, pepper chicken, mutton fries, and nati-style saaru to eat with ragi mudde. Eat at the table or take it home.

What makes a donne biryani

  1. 01

    Seeraga samba rice

    Short-grain, fragrant rice that soaks up the masala instead of sitting on top of it.

  2. 02

    Green masala

    Mint, coriander and green chilli in place of a heavy red gravy.

  3. 03

    The donne

    A cup folded from dried leaves. It holds the heat and adds a faint earthy note.

  4. 04

    Kabab on the side

    Bengaluru's standard pairing: a plate of crisp red chicken kabab.
